★ Outstanding Poster Award · 2026 Capstone Design Project with ETRI · Mar – Jun 2026

Trust Gateway: A Zero Trust Proxy for Dynamic Trust Evaluation and Re-authentication

A proxy in front of OWASP Juice Shop that recomputes a per-request trust score (0–1) from 24 behavioral features grouped into 6 interpretable signals, then maps it to Allow, Monitor, Re-authenticate, or Block. Signal weights are tuned with SLSQP on 15,721 requests from 410 sessions.

Team project with Huijeong Choi, Jia Hong, and Dongseok Kim

Poster
Control Theory and Applications · Spring 2025

Frequency Stabilization for Turbine Systems

Coupled steam–gas turbines with 5% droop had a 1.26° phase margin. Compensators (PM 40–60°, GM ≥ 6 dB) plus AGC restored stable frequency.
